Lexis
Android vocabulary app: a personal word database, fast capture, flashcards, and home screen widgets.

Một hướng visual duy nhất, làm lại từ đầu: sans-serif (Be Vietnam Pro + Noto cho IPA, Kana, Hanzi), nền trung tính lạnh, một màu nhấn indigo trầm, phân cấp bằng tông surface của Material 3 thay vì viền. Mọi điện thoại trên trang đều là prototype bấm được.

Flow chính, thử trên điện thoại bên phải
1. Home → Add a word… → gõ perseverance → Save → bấm từ vừa lưu
2. Home → See all → IELTS 3000 → bấm một từ → vuốt ngang để sang từ khác
3. Home → Review → Show answer → swipe or tap a rating … → Done
4. Home → On your widget → Create widget → Add to Home Screen
5. Decks → Import → oxford_10k.csv → Import → Go to deck

S21+Widget styles · explorationSix other treatments for the same words. All interactive.
W1Flip card
Self-test on the home screen. The word shows first; tap to turn the card, then rate yourself. Again and Got it feed the review schedule.
W2Quick check
One question, three meanings. The answer counts as a review, so glancing at the phone becomes practice.
W3Glyph tile
2×2 for Kanji and Hanzi. One character fills the tile; reading and meaning sit underneath. Tinted with the primary container. Tap for the next one.
W4Clear
No background. The word sits on the wallpaper like the clock does, for people who want a quiet home screen.
W5Dictionary page
From the Classical system: serif headword, italic IPA, a numbered sense, one gold hairline and outlined buttons, matted like a printed page.
W6Daily ring
Today’s goal as a ring, the words due, and one Review button. The next word is named so the number has a face.
