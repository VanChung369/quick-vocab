A stress mark and an L. The first thing you learn about a word is how to say it.

The IPA primary-stress mark
It sits before the stressed syllable, as in /ˈlɛksɪs/. It ties the mark to pronunciation, which the app adds to every word.

A caret and a baseline
The upright reads as a text cursor (adding a word), the foot as the underline that marks a word inside an example sentence.
